Bit Bowl Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

Bit Bowl is a bowling game. It has no ads, no accounts and no tracking, and we don't sell or share your data.

What stays on your phone

Your bowlers' initials and looks, high score, unlocked items, sound setting and lane choice are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Pro Shop purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat, a purchase-verification service, to confirm what you bought. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app ID and your purchase records (which items and when). It does not receive your name, email or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center under your Game Center profile. Apple's privacy policy covers that data.

Game updates

The app checks for small game updates by downloading files from GitHub Pages. Like any website, GitHub may log basic request information such as IP address. No personal information is sent. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Bit Bowl doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Questions: james.hollers@gmail.com
