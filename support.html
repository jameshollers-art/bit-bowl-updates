How to play

Move your bowler with the arrow buttons. Pull down on the ball pad, then flick up to roll. A harder flick rolls faster. Flick up and to one side to curve the ball, and swipe the pad while the ball rolls to add more hook.

Multiplayer

Choose 2P, 3P or 4P on the title screen and pass the phone around. Turns rotate each frame.

Pro Shop purchases

Pro Shop items are one-time purchases that stay yours. To get them back on a new phone or after reinstalling, open Locker Room → Pro Shop and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem page.

Game Center

Sign in to Game Center in your iPhone's Settings to post scores to the leaderboards and earn achievements. Bit Bowl works fine without it.
